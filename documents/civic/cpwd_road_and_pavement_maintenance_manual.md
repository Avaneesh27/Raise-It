# CPWD & Municipal Road Repair Technical Guidelines & SOP

**Document ID:** CPWD-RD-SOP-2024-03  
**Issuing Authority:** Central Public Works Department & Indian Roads Congress (IRC)  
**Applicability:** Municipal Roads, Arterial Corridors, Residential Colony Pavements  
**Jurisdiction:** Municipal Corporation Engineering Wing (Roads Division)  
**Effective Year:** 2024–2026  

---

## 1. Defect Classification & Hazard Severity

Road surface defects are categorized into three operational classes:

1. **Class A - Critical Road Cavity / Sinkhole:**
   - *Definition:* Pothole depth exceeding 75mm (3 inches) or width exceeding 0.5 meters, or any structural road collapse threatening vehicular overturn or two-wheeler skids.
   - *Emergency Action:* Immediate safety cones and retroreflective barricading within **2 hours**.
   - *Repair Window:* Compulsory cold-mix asphalt patch within **24 hours**. Permanent hot-mix restoration within **7 days**.

2. **Class B - Standard Surface Pothole & Edge Raveling:**
   - *Definition:* Surface depressions between 25mm and 75mm depth resulting from water ingress or heavy axle loads.
   - *Repair Window:* Rectification within **48 to 72 hours** during dry season, or max **5 working days** under inclement weather conditions.

3. **Class C - Minor Alligator Cracking & Rutting:**
   - *Definition:* Micro-cracking network without immediate loss of sub-base material.
   - *Repair Window:* Slurry seal or microsurfacing scheduled during preventive quarterly maintenance runs (within **15 to 30 days**).

## 2. Technical Repair Protocol for Road Engineers

When rectifying pothole complaints:
- **Step 1 (Excavation):** The defective area must be cut into a clean geometric shape (square or rectangular) with vertical edges down to solid base material.
- **Step 2 (Debris Clearance):** All loose dust, stone aggregate, and stagnant water must be removed using an air blower or wire brush.
- **Step 3 (Tack Coat Application):** Bitumen emulsion (RS-1 or SS-1 grade) must be applied uniformly to the bottom and vertical edges at 0.25 to 0.30 kg/sqm.
- **Step 4 (Filling):** High-performance polymer-modified cold mix (IRC:SP:100 compliant) must be used during monsoons, and dense bituminous macadam (DBM) during dry weather.
- **Step 5 (Compaction):** Mechanical vibratory plate compactor (or 8-10 tonne roller for large patches) must achieve at least 98% laboratory density flush with the existing road surface.
- **Step 6 (Verification):** Digital geo-tagged photographic evidence must be recorded before opening the lane to traffic.

---

## 3. Jurisdiction & Boundary Demarcation

- **National Highways (NHAI) Passing Through Municipal Limits:** Maintained by NHAI Project Implementation Unit (PIU). Complaints are forwarded via inter-agency protocol within 24 hours.
- **Major District Roads & State Highways (PWD):** Maintained by State Public Works Department (PWD).
- **Internal Municipal Roads, Colony Streets, and Ward Lanes:** Exclusively maintained by the Municipal Corporation Executive Engineer (Roads).
- **Private Layouts / Gated Enclaves:** Internal repairs are the responsibility of the Resident Welfare Association (RWA) or developer until the layout is officially handed over to the Municipal Corporation through a registered deed.
