# Solid Waste Management & Public Sanitation Protocol

**Document ID:** MUNI-SWM-POL-2024-04
**Department:** Sanitation & Public Health Department
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** November 2024

## 1. Purpose & Jurisdiction
This protocol prescribes guidelines for handling domestic garbage accumulation, open dump sites, overflowing public bins, commercial waste violations, and animal carcass removal within municipal boundaries.

## 2. Waste Classification & Service Level Agreements (SLA)
### 2.1 Black Spots & Unattended Garbage
- **Residential Overflow:** Bins or piles obstructing sidewalks or streets must be cleared by the motorized collection team within 24 hours of report.
- **Commercial Dumping / Construction Debris:** Commercial waste dumping attracts a fine under Bylaw 14. Sanitation inspector must inspect within 12 hours and schedule mechanical loader clearance within 36 hours.
- **Biomedical or Hazardous Waste:** Immediate cordon off within 2 hours; authorized biohazard contractor dispatch within 6 hours.

### 2.2 Recurring Sanitation Hotspots
Any location with more than 3 complaints in 14 days is designated a "Vulnerable Garbage Blackspot". The Sanitation Inspector must:
1. Conduct nighttime surveillance against illegal dumping.
2. Recommend installation of high-capacity closed bins or CCTV surveillance.
3. Intensify door-to-door waste collection frequency to twice daily.

## 3. Complaint Resolution Protocol
1. **Sanitation Supervisor Dispatch:** The supervisor of the sanitary ward receives the GPS coordinates and photo.
2. **Collection & Disinfection:** The waste is loaded onto compactor vehicles, and the spot is treated with lime powder or disinfectant spray.
3. **Resolution Evidence:** A clean site photo is captured and verified before marking the report as RESOLVED.
