## 1. Statutory Service Level Agreement (SLA) Matrix

Under the Swachh Bharat Mission (Urban) Grievance Redressal Rules, every Municipal Corporation is bound by legally mandated resolution timelines:

| Civic Issue Category | Initial Action By Official | Maximum Resolution SLA | Assigned Ward Official | Escalation Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Garbage Dump / Overflowing Dustbin** | Within 6 hours | 12 hours | Sanitary Inspector (SI) | +12 hours past SLA → Health Officer |
| **Dead Animals Removal** | Within 2 hours | 48 hours | Sanitation Vector Control Unit | +24 hours past SLA → Chief Medical Officer |
| **Open Manhole / Damaged Sewer Lid** | Immediate Barricading (within 1 hour) | 12 hours | Junior Engineer (JE Sewerage) | +6 hours past SLA → Executive Engineer |
| **Blocked Public Drain / Sewer Overflow** | Within 4 hours | 24 hours | Stormwater & Drainage Section | +12 hours past SLA → Ward Executive Engineer |
| **Pothole on Major Road / Cave-in** | Within 4 hours (safety warning) | 48 hours (temporary patch) / 7 days (permanent) | Assistant Engineer (PHE/Roads) | +24 hours past SLA → Superintending Engineer |
| **No Water Supply / Pipe Burst** | Within 2 hours | 12 hours (pipe burst) / 24 hours (supply restoration) | Water Supply & Sewerage Board | +12 hours past SLA → Chief Engineer |
| **Streetlight Outage / Dark Spot** | Within 12 hours | 24 to 48 hours | Electrical Department Engineer | +48 hours past SLA → Zonal Commissioner |

## 2. Compulsory Evidence & Verification Protocol

1. **Geotagged photographic intake:** All citizen complaints must automatically record GPS coordinates (WGS84) and timestamp.
2. **Mandatory Photographic Proof of Resolution:** Field officers or junior engineers cannot mark any ticket as "RESOLVED" without uploading an after-repair photograph taken within 50 meters of the original geotagged location.
3. **Citizen Reopen Right:** If a citizen is dissatisfied with the work done, they have the statutory right to reopen the grievance ticket within 48 hours of the "RESOLVED" notification.
4. **Duplicate Hotspot Clustering:** If 3 or more complaints arrive within a 500-meter radius for the same issue category within 14 days, the system automatically marks the site as an "Active Hotspot", doubling the priority score and mandating inspection by the Ward Executive Engineer.

## 3. Four-Tier Municipal Escalation Hierarchy

If a registered complaint exceeds the prescribed SLA without resolution, the following escalation is enforced automatically:

- **Tier 1 (At SLA Expiry):** Automated notification and penalty alert sent to the designated Ward Junior Engineer / Sanitary Inspector.
- **Tier 2 (+24 to +48 hours past SLA):** Grievance escalates directly to the Zonal Executive Engineer and Municipal Health Officer.
- **Tier 3 (+5 days past SLA):** Ticket placed on the Municipal Commissioner’s Weekly Priority Review Dashboard.
- **Tier 4 (Unresolved past 7 days / Repeated Failures):** Citizen can invoke the Right to Public Services Act (RTS), request a formal hearing before the Ombudsman / Lokayukta, or file a complaint with the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

## 4. Municipal Department Responsibilities Directory

- **Solid Waste Management Department:** Door-to-door domestic waste collection, commercial market cleaning, clearance of roadside dumps, bio-methanation plants, and littering penalties under Bye-laws.
- **Roads & Traffic Infrastructure Department:** Asphalt macadam road repairs, pothole filling, sidewalk pavement maintenance, road dividers, speed breaker marking, and storm culverts along roadways.
- **Water Supply & Sewerage Board:** Piped potable water distribution, trunk line leak repairs, water pressure equalization, underground sewerage desilting, and sewage treatment plants (STPs).
- **Public Lighting Section (Electrical):** High-mast tower lamps, street light LED fixtures, photocell/timer replacements, exposed underground cable rectifications, and electrical feeder box safety.
- **Health & Vector Control Cell:** Fogging operations for dengue/malaria, open drainage disinfection, stray animal carcass disposal, and public toilet inspections.
