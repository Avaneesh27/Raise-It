# Municipal Corporation: Standard Operating Procedure for Road Maintenance & Pothole Repair

**Document ID:** MUNI-RDS-SOP-2025-01
**Department:** Roads & Infrastructure Department
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** January 2025

## 1. Scope & Objective
This Standard Operating Procedure governs the inspection, categorization, prioritization, and repair of asphalt and concrete roadway defects across all municipal wards, including potholes, crater formation, surface deterioration, and cave-ins.

## 2. Issue Categorization & Response Timelines
### 2.1 Pothole Classification
- **Minor Potholes (Depth < 5 cm, Width < 30 cm):** Standard priority. Target inspection within 48 hours; asphalt patch repair within 5 working days.
- **Moderate Potholes (Depth 5–10 cm, Width 30–60 cm):** Elevated priority. Target inspection within 24 hours; repair within 72 hours.
- **Severe Potholes / Cave-ins (Depth > 10 cm, Width > 60 cm):** Critical emergency priority. Immediate physical barricading within 4 hours; emergency cold-mix filling within 12 hours, followed by permanent hot-mix repair within 48 hours.

### 2.2 Recurring Pothole Clusters
When 3 or more distinct pothole reports are recorded within a 500-meter radius within a 30-day period, the zone is classified as a "Recurring Road Failure Cluster". This triggers an engineering structural assessment by the Assistant Executive Engineer (Roads) to evaluate sub-base drainage failure rather than isolated patching.

## 3. Citizen Complaint Handling Workflow
1. **Receipt & Automated Logging:** The complaint is registered on the municipal portal and allocated to the Ward Junior Engineer (Roads).
2. **Field Verification:** A field team inspects the coordinate location, verifies photographic evidence, and records road classification (arterial, sub-arterial, or residential).
3. **Work Order Generation:** Contractor or direct department road maintenance team is assigned a digital work order.
4. **Execution & Photographic Proof:** Road repair contractor completes the patch work. A timestamped resolution photograph of the completed repair must be uploaded before the ticket can be marked as RESOLVED.

## 4. Escalation Matrix
- **Level 1:** Ward Junior Engineer (Resolution within 72 hours).
- **Level 2:** Assistant Executive Engineer (Roads) if pending > 5 days. Contact: roads-escalation@municipal.gov
- **Level 3:** Chief Municipal Engineer (Infrastructure) for unaddressed cluster issues exceeding 10 days.
