# Public Lighting, Streetlight Infrastructure & Electrical Safety Guidelines

**Document ID:** MUNI-ELEC-SOP-2024-09
**Department:** Electrical Engineering & Public Lighting Department
**Version:** 1.8
**Effective Date:** August 2024

## 1. Overview
Governs the maintenance, replacement, and emergency safety interventions regarding streetlights, high-mast lamps, junction feeder pillars, and exposed wiring on municipal public roads.

## 2. Issue Severity & Repair Timelines
- **Individual Fixture Failure (Burned Lamp / Faulty Driver):** Replaced within 48 hours of ticket logging.
- **Dark Stretch (3 or more consecutive streetlights unlit):** Elevated safety risk. Mandatory inspection and restoration within 24 hours.
- **Live Wire / Exposed Electrical Hazard / Collapsed Pole:** Critical Life Safety Emergency. Rapid response team dispatched within 60 minutes. Power isolated immediately, physical cordoning implemented, and repairs executed within 6 hours.

## 3. Department Procedure for Complaint Handling
1. **Verification & Circuit Identification:** Electrical Assistant Engineer cross-references pole number or GPS tag with the Central Streetlight Automation Feeder database.
2. **Work Order Assignment:** Maintenance van equipped with hydraulic lift and spare LED luminaires dispatched.
3. **Closing Ticket:** Engineer uploads night-time illuminated photo or proof of physical fixture replacement.
