# Stormwater Drainage & Monsoon Waterlogging Management SOP

**Document ID:** MUNI-DRN-SOP-2024-05
**Department:** Stormwater Drainage & Flood Control Department
**Version:** 2.3
**Effective Date:** May 2024

## 1. Scope
Covers open stormwater drains, subterranean box drains, roadside culverts, and sewer-storm cross-connections.

## 2. Issues & SLAs
- **Missing or Broken Manhole/Drain Cover:** Critical Life Hazard. Barricade placed within 2 hours. Heavy-duty cast iron or SFRC cover replaced within 8 hours.
- **Silt Blockage / Water stagnation:** Silt removal and suction de-silting deployed within 48 hours.
- **Severe Street Inundation / Flooding:** Mobile diesel pumps (100 HP) dispatched within 90 minutes.

## 3. Recurring Overflow Clusters
Locations with recurring drainage overflows (2 or more reports within 15 days) are queued for immediate hydraulic gradient analysis and de-silting by the executive engineering division.
