# Citizen Complaint Lifecycle & Escalation Charter

**Document ID:** MUNI-GOV-CHR-2025-01
**Department:** Municipal Administration & Citizen Grievance Cell
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 2025

## 1. The Citizen Grievance Lifecycle
Every complaint submitted through the RaiseIt platform progresses through a transparent and auditable 5-stage lifecycle:
1. **SUBMITTED:** The complaint has been registered in the municipal database, assigned a unique tracking ID (e.g., #RI1024), and routed to the competent department based on issue category.
2. **UNDER_REVIEW:** The ward supervisor or junior engineer reviews the photographic evidence, confirms jurisdiction, and checks for duplicate/cluster reports.
3. **ASSIGNED:** A specific field officer, maintenance crew, or authorized contractor has been assigned with a work order.
4. **IN_PROGRESS:** Active physical resolution work is taking place at the location. Progress notes and photos are posted to the timeline.
5. **RESOLVED:** Physical work has concluded. The officer attaches proof of resolution, which the citizen can inspect.
6. **REJECTED (Exception):** Applied only if the issue falls outside municipal jurisdiction (e.g. private property), or is an exact duplicate ticket already addressed.

## 2. Citizen Rights & Timeline Commitments
- **Acknowledgment:** Instant digital acknowledgment and tracking code upon submission.
- **Initial Review:** Under review status updated within 24 hours of submission.
- **Average Resolution Time:** 3 to 7 working days depending on issue severity, weather conditions, and structural requirements.

## 3. Escalation Procedure
If a complaint remains unresolved past the department Service Level Agreement:
- **First Escalation (+3 days past SLA):** Notification sent automatically to the Ward Executive Engineer.
- **Second Escalation (+7 days past SLA):** Complaint flagged on the Municipal Commissioner's Priority Grievance Board.
- **Public Grievance Redressal Officer:** Citizens can request a status hearing via the Grievance Cell at municipal-help@gov.in or helpline 1800-CIVIC-FIX.
