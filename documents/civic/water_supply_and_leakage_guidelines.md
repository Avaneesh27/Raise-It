# Municipal Water Supply & Pipeline Leakage Guidelines

**Document ID:** MUNI-WAT-SOP-2025-02
**Department:** Water Supply & Sewerage Board
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** February 2025

## 1. Principles & Objectives
To mitigate drinking water wastage, prevent pressure drops in the municipal pipeline network, and safeguard potable water against contamination caused by underground supply pipe breaches.

## 2. Leakage Classification & Response Matrix
- **Minor Joint Seepage (No waterlogging, dripping valve):** Assessed within 48 hours; repair scheduled during standard maintenance cycling within 5 days.
- **Moderate Main Pipe Rupture (Visible surface flow, localized pooling):** Valve isolation within 6 hours to prevent loss; pipeline excavation and clamp welding completed within 24 hours.
- **Major Trunk Line Burst (Gushing water, road undermining, severe low pressure):** High-priority emergency. Water Board quick response team dispatched within 45 minutes. Upstream gate valves shut immediately; repair crew dispatched with heavy backhoe loaders. Target restoration within 12 hours.

## 3. Water Contamination Protocol
If dirty or foul-smelling tap water is reported, water supply to the sub-zone is paused immediately. Chlorination shock dosing and water sample bacteriological testing conducted before resuming supply.
